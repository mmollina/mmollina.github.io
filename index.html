Genetic mapping · Polyploids

Multi-family linkage maps in polyploids

Breeding programs often work with several related crosses. MAPpoly2 combines information across interconnected diploid and autopolyploid families to build integrated linkage maps. It extends the single-family models in MAPpoly.

MAPpoly2 on GitHub MAPpoly method (G3, 2019)

Creator of MAPpoly and MAPpoly2.

How MAPpoly2 builds the map

A hidden Markov model builds a map for each parent. These are combined into a joint map, which is then recomputed to place the remaining markers. Ploidy levels 2, 4, and 6 can be combined in any configuration, and the computationally intensive steps are written in C++.

Example output: a potato consensus map

Recent methods development

Maternal maps from open-pollinated families

In open-pollinated families the mother is known and genotyped, but the fathers are not observed. HalfSibMap is an R package that builds maternal linkage maps and reconstructs each offspring's maternal inheritance in diploid open-pollinated families, using the offspring genotypes and the genotyped mother.

Role
Author and maintainer, with co-authors Kim McKeever, Molly Irvin, and Fikret Isik.
Status
Available from GitHub; a methodological manuscript is in preparation.
Code
HalfSibMap on GitHub
Method details

A source-aware hidden Markov model pairs the transmitted maternal homolog with a paternal source state. Persistent paternal haplotype-sharing tracts, as arise when some pollen parents are related to the mother, are then modelled explicitly rather than absorbed into the maternal recombination map. These states describe shared tracts statistically; they do not identify fathers. Intervals without linkage are reported as gaps rather than as large distances.

Genomics · Sweetpotato

Inheritance and genome architecture of hexaploid sweetpotato

Hexaploid sweetpotato has six sets of chromosomes, making inheritance analysis more complex. Ultra-dense multilocus mapping in a full-sib population helped clarify it. Related work includes a chapter on genetic maps in sweetpotato and, as a co-author, a phased chromosome-level genome assembly.

Inheritance (G3, 2020) Genome assembly (Nature Plants, 2025) Genetic maps in sweetpotato (2024)

Visualization

Exploring polyploid genetic analyses in one place

Results from linkage, QTL, and genome analyses often sit in separate tools. VIEWpoly is an interactive application that brings them together, so users can explore candidate genes in a genome browser. It reads output from MAPpoly, polymapR, diaQTL, QTLpoly, and polyqtlR.

About

I am a quantitative geneticist trained in plant genetics and breeding. My doctoral work at the University of São Paulo developed models for building genetic maps in autopolyploids, with applications in sugarcane. Since then I have focused on algorithms for genomic analysis in complex family structures, especially outcrossing diploid and polyploid crops.

At North Carolina State University I worked with breeders and collaborators across several species, combining statistical models with R and C++ software.

At AbacusBio, I work on genetic evaluation, genotype data quality, and statistical analysis for breeding programs, developing reproducible workflows and communicating results to project teams.
